The NSX community is an essential part of the ownership experience.

## Community resources

1. **[NSX Prime Forums](https://www.nsxprime.com/)**  
   The original forums. Years of accumulated knowledge, technical discussion, and ownership experience.
2. **[NSX Prime (Facebook)](https://www.facebook.com/groups/7990613243)**  
   Conversation and connections from the wider Prime community.
3. **[NSX Owners Group (Facebook)](https://www.facebook.com/groups/nsxowners)**  
   Connect with owners, share experiences, and ask questions.
4. **[NSXoTX (Facebook)](https://www.facebook.com/groups/361677363979527/)**  
   Our Texas NSX community. Introduce yourself and find your people nearby.
5. **[NSX Club of America](https://nsxca.org/)**  
   Regional connections, events, and *NSX Driver* magazine.
6. **[NSXPO](https://nsxca.org/events)**  
   The club’s annual gathering for track time and social events. Make it to one.
